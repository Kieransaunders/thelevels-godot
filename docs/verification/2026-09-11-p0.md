## Known defects and accepted deviations (release record)

Source defects preserved by design (see also §P2 and §P5):
- Ember-drop budget overshoot: a strike/pour can spend up to 0.5 ember beyond the buffer's contents.
- Fire RNG is not reseeded by reset, so post-reset burn patterns differ from the first run.
- Druid steering probes only the 2.4 m endpoint, not the segment: hazards narrower than the probe are entered, not avoided.
- Earth-scoop groundwater seeps water from nothing below the 1.2 m table.

Accepted port deviations:
- Deterministic replacement noise changes terrain and fuel maps from the Unity original (ADR).
- Water mesh vertex colours come back 8-bit quantized (flow channel tolerance 1.5/255 in the gate).
- The brush ring is a 1-px line primitive and reads as a thin line at grazing angles.
- Druids keep walking while water/fire are paused (source behaviour, asserted by gate).
- An occluded macOS window throttles to ~1 fps; verification captures pin the window on top to stay honest.

Deferred, not defects:
- Signed standalone `.app` export (needs Godot export templates).
- P7's "50-action scenario" drives the sim API directly rather than synthesized mouse clicks; the Input Map path is covered by `--verify-input` and the P4 gate.
